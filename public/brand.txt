[naming-rules]
Always capitalise each word: "Pathway Academy Zone".
"PAZ" is acceptable as a short form on second mention only; spell out the full name on first mention.
Do not insert "the" before the name (write "Pathway Academy Zone delivers...", not "the Pathway Academy Zone delivers...").
Do not pluralise: it is "Pathway", not "Pathways".
Do not describe as a "school" or "PRU" — the correct descriptor is "Alternative Provision" or "Alternative Provision provider".
When referring to the parent organisation, use "Pathway Group" — these are distinct entities.

[brand-voice]
Tone: warm, professional, plain English. Avoid jargon and acronyms without explanation.
Audience: school leaders, SENCOs, behaviour leads, Local Authority commissioners, parents and carers.
Style: factual, evidence-led, child-centred. Avoid superlatives and marketing hyperbole.
Accessibility: write for a reading age of 14-16 where possible.

[boilerplate]
Pathway Academy Zone is an Alternative Provision based in Stoke-on-Trent, supporting young people aged 11-16 across Staffordshire who need a different pathway to succeed. Working with schools, Multi-Academy Trusts and Local Authorities, Pathway Academy Zone delivers academic, vocational, therapeutic and life-skills programmes that improve behaviour, attendance and outcomes.
