Q: What age range does Pathway Academy Zone support?
A: We support young people aged 11–16, primarily in Key Stages 3 and 4.

Q: Where is Pathway Academy Zone located?
A: Our centre is located at Duncalf St, Burslem, Stoke-on-Trent, ST6 3LJ.

Q: What are the core programmes offered at PAZ?
A: We offer six core programmes: Academic Re-engagement, Vocational Learning, SEMH Support, Personal Development, Life Skills, and Employability Skills.

Q: Is transport provided for students?
A: We work closely with referring schools and local authorities to coordinate transport arrangements where needed.

Q: How can a school make a referral?
A: Referrals can be made via our website's referral form or by contacting our partnerships team at info@pathwayacademyzone.co.uk.

Q: What is the maximum group size in classes?
A: We maintain small group sizes, typically with a maximum of 6 students per group, to ensure high levels of individual support.

Q: Are your programmes accredited?
A: Yes, we offer accredited pathways including functional skills and GCSEs through recognised awarding bodies.
