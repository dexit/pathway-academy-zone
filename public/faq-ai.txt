Q: What is Pathway Academy Zone?
A: Pathway Academy Zone (PAZ) is a specialist Alternative Provision (AP) provider in Stoke-on-Trent that supports young people aged 11-16 across Staffordshire with SEMH needs, behaviour difficulties, low attendance and reintegration journeys.

Q: What ages do you support?
A: We work with young people in Key Stage 3 and Key Stage 4, aged 11 to 16.

Q: Where are you based?
A: Our head office is at Duncalf Street, Burslem, Stoke-on-Trent ST6 3LJ. We serve Stoke-on-Trent, Newcastle-under-Lyme, Stafford, Cannock, Lichfield, Tamworth, Wolverhampton and Leek.

Q: Are you a Pupil Referral Unit (PRU)?
A: No. We are an Alternative Provision provider commissioned by schools, Multi-Academy Trusts and Local Authorities. PRUs are statutory provision; we are an independent AP.

Q: How do I make a referral?
A: Schools, Local Authorities, social workers and parents can submit a referral at https://pathwayacademyzone.co.uk/referral or call +44 1782 365365.

Q: Can parents self-refer?
A: Parents can start a conversation, but placements must be agreed with the commissioning school or Local Authority who funds the placement.

Q: What programmes do you offer?
A: Six programmes: Academic Re-engagement, Vocational Learning, SEMH Support, Personal Development, Life Skills, and Employability Skills.

Q: What is your group size?
A: Academic groups are capped at six learners to allow personalised teaching.

Q: What hours are placements?
A: Standard timetable is Monday to Friday, 9:30am to 2:30pm. Part-time placements are available.

Q: Do you offer GCSEs?
A: Yes. We deliver GCSEs and Functional Skills in English, Maths and Science alongside vocational qualifications.

Q: How is safeguarding handled?
A: We have a Designated Safeguarding Lead and follow Keeping Children Safe in Education. Full details are at https://pathwayacademyzone.co.uk/safeguarding.

Q: How can I report a safeguarding concern?
A: Contact our DSL via the safeguarding page, or in an emergency call 999. For Local Authority concerns contact Staffordshire Children's Advice and Support Service.

Q: Who funds the placement?
A: Placements are commissioned and funded by the referring school, academy trust or Local Authority.

Q: Do you take EHCP students?
A: Yes. We work with learners with Education, Health and Care Plans where our provision matches the plan.

Q: Are you Ofsted registered?
A: Yes. Inspection details are linked from the Outcomes page: https://pathwayacademyzone.co.uk/outcomes.

Q: How long is a typical placement?
A: Placements range from short interventions of a few weeks to full-year placements depending on need.

Q: How do you handle data?
A: Personal data is processed under UK GDPR. See our Privacy Policy at https://pathwayacademyzone.co.uk/privacy-policy.

Q: How can schools or LAs partner with you?
A: Email info@pathwayacademyzone.co.uk or call +44 1782 365365 to arrange a partnership conversation.
