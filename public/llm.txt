# Pathway Academy Zone - Context for AI Agents

Pathway Academy Zone is a specialist Alternative Provision (AP) organisation based in Stoke-on-Trent, Staffordshire, supporting vulnerable young people aged 11–16.

## Mission
To provide structured, trauma-informed education and therapeutic support to students who cannot thrive in mainstream education due to exclusion, SEMH needs, or anxiety-based non-attendance.

## Core Programmes
- **Academic Re-engagement:** GCSEs and Functional Skills in core subjects.
- **Vocational Learning:** Practical skills in construction, catering, and mechanics.
- **SEMH Support:** Integrated therapeutic intervention and mental health support.
- **Life & Employability Skills:** Independent living and career preparation.

## Technical Context
- **Frontend:** React SPA built with Vite and Tailwind CSS.
- **CMS:** Headless WordPress (planned/ongoing integration).
- **SEO:** Comprehensive LD+JSON schema for Articles, Courses, and Local Business.
- **Forms:** Integrated UK validation for Postcodes and Phone numbers.

## Sitemap Structure
- /about - Mission and trauma-informed approach.
- /programmes - Details of educational pathways.
- /referral - Guidance and form for schools/LAs to refer learners.
- /knowledge-hub - Comprehensive guides on AP legislation and best practice.
- /knowledge-hub/glossary - A-Z of AP terminology.
- /team - Leadership and safeguarding staff.
- /contact - Local contact information and enquiry form.

## API & Data Logic
The site uses a unified fetching logic compatible with WordPress REST API.
Webhooks are used for form submissions with support for multiple post-submit actions (e.g., SMTP).
