## Sections

### Programme 1: Academic Re-engagement

- **Tag:** Core Programme
- **Heading:** Academic Re-engagement
- **Body:** Structured academic curriculum adapted for individual learning needs, focusing on core subjects and building confidence in learning.
- **What's included:**
  - English, Maths & Science
  - PSHE / RSHE, RE, PE & Citizenship
  - Small group teaching (max 6)
  - Personalised learning plans
- **Schedule:** Full-time or part-time placements
- **Time:** Monday to Friday, 9:30am – 2:30pm
- **Who is it for?** Students following permanent exclusion, at risk of exclusion, or with significant attendance issues.
- **Outcomes:** Improved attendance · GCSE / functional skills · Confidence in learning
- **Image:** `classroom-1-NBeOjKjJ.webp` (programmes folder)

### Programme 2: Vocational Learning

- **Tag:** Skills & Careers
- **Heading:** Vocational Learning
- **Body:** Hands-on practical programmes developing real-world skills in areas like construction, catering, motor mechanics, and horticulture.
- **What's included:**
  - Industry-standard training
  - Work experience placements
  - Recognised qualifications
  - Employer partnerships
- **Schedule:** 1–2 days per week alongside academic
- **Time:** Varies by programme
- **Who is it for?** Young people who thrive with practical, hands-on learning and are working towards vocational qualifications.
- **Outcomes:** Industry certificates · Work experience · Apprenticeship pathways
- **Image:** `training-wall-uqB92baC.webp` (programmes folder)

### Programme 3: SEMH Support

- **Tag:** Therapeutic
- **Heading:** SEMH Support
- **Body:** Therapeutic intervention and support for young people with social, emotional, and mental health needs, integrated throughout all provision.
- **What's included:**
  - 1:1 therapeutic sessions
  - Group workshops
  - Emotional regulation support
  - Family support sessions
- **Schedule:** Ongoing throughout placement
- **Time:** Typically 2–3 sessions per week
- **Who is it for?** Any student whose SEMH needs are a barrier to engagement or whose EHCP identifies therapeutic support.
- **Outcomes:** Improved emotional regulation · Reduced anxiety · Stronger relationships
- **Image:** `reception-area-CTi6XeEj.webp` (programmes folder)

### Programme 4: Personal Development

- **Tag:** Enrichment
- **Heading:** Personal Development
- **Body:** Building essential life skills, resilience, and self-esteem through structured programmes and enrichment activities.
- **What's included:**
  - Communication skills
  - Problem-solving
  - Team building
  - Goal setting
- **Schedule:** Integrated into weekly timetable
- **Time:** 2 hours per week minimum
- **Who is it for?** All students — woven into every programme to build the foundations young people need for life.
- **Outcomes:** Self-esteem · Resilience · Team skills
- **Image:** `skills-advice-BsX137y0.webp` (programmes folder)

### Programme 5: Life Skills Programme

- **Tag:** Independence
- **Heading:** Life Skills Programme
- **Body:** Practical skills for independent living including budgeting, cooking, health and wellbeing, and managing relationships.
- **What's included:**
  - Cooking & nutrition
  - Financial literacy
  - Health education
  - Digital skills including AI skills
- **Schedule:** Integrated into curriculum
- **Time:** Weekly sessions
- **Who is it for?** Particularly valuable for CLA, young people with EHCPs, and those at risk of NEET post-16.
- **Outcomes:** Independent living skills · Digital literacy · Health awareness
- **Image:** `amazon-careers-BRVYfevZ.webp` (programmes folder)

### Programme 6: Employability Skills

- **Tag:** Post-16 Ready
- **Heading:** Employability Skills
- **Body:** Preparing young people for the world of work through CV writing, interview practice, and understanding workplace expectations.
- **What's included:**
  - CV & application support
  - Interview preparation
  - Work experience
  - Careers guidance
- **Schedule:** Year 10 & 11 focus
- **Time:** Weekly sessions plus placements
- **Who is it for?** Year 10 and 11 students preparing for college, apprenticeships, or employment.
- **Outcomes:** Employment-ready · Interview confidence · Positive destinations
- **Image:** `careers-fair-B2B2seve.webp` (programmes folder)

### How Our Programmes Work Together

- **Label:** Integrated Approach
- **Heading:** How Our Programmes Work Together
- **Body:** No young person receives a single programme in isolation. Every placement is a blend designed around individual need.

**Integration layers:**

| Title | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| Safeguarding & Pastoral Foundation | Every young person has a named key worker. Safeguarding, wellbeing and relationships underpin every session. |
| Academic Core | English, maths and wider curriculum delivered in groups of up to 6, with individual learning plans that flex as students progress. |
| Vocational & Enrichment Layer | Practical programmes, life skills and employability woven around the academic core to build real-world readiness. |
| Therapeutic Support | SEMH interventions run in parallel — 1:1 sessions, group work, emotional regulation and family liaison. |
| Progress & Review | Formal half-termly reviews with the young person, family and referring school. Targets adjusted as confidence grows. |
| Destinations Planning | Post-16 planning begins at the first review. College applications, apprenticeship research and employer introductions. |

### Where We Deliver

- **Label:** Where We Deliver
- **Heading:** Serving Schools Across Staffordshire
- **Body:** All programmes are delivered from our purpose-built centre at Duncalf Street, Burslem, Stoke-on-Trent. We accept referrals from schools and local authorities across the region — including Stafford, Newcastle-under-Lyme, Cannock, Lichfield, Tamworth and Wolverhampton.
- **Address:** Duncalf St, Burslem ST6 3LJ
- **Hours:** Mon–Fri, 8:30am–4:00pm

**Placement info cards:**
- **Emergency Placements:** Students can start within 48 hours following an emergency referral — we work fast when young people need support urgently.
- **Standard Placements:** Comprehensive assessment and planning typically means a student starts within 1–2 weeks of referral, with a fully personalised timetable in place from day one.

**Area links:**
- Stoke-on-Trent, Newcastle-under-Lyme, Stafford, Cannock, Lichfield, Tamworth, Wolverhampton
