### Our Commitment

- **Heading:** Our Commitment
- **Body:** At Pathway Academy Zone, we recognise that young people in Alternative Provision may be particularly vulnerable. Our approach to safeguarding is proactive, relational, and trauma-informed.

**Commitments:**
1. All staff complete enhanced DBS checks and receive regular safeguarding training
2. We maintain clear reporting procedures and work closely with local safeguarding partners
3. Young people are taught to recognise risks and know who to talk to if they feel unsafe
