### What a Placement Looks Like (Journey Timeline)

- **Label:** The Journey
- **Heading:** What a Placement Looks Like
- **Body:** From first contact to positive destination — a typical pathway through our provision.

**Journey steps:**

| Step | Title | Description |
|------|-------|-------------|
| 1 | Referral | School, LA or social worker contacts us. We assess the young person's needs and agree a placement plan. |
| 2 | Assessment | Comprehensive assessment across academic, social, emotional and vocational areas. Baseline established. |
| 3 | Programme Start | Personalised timetable begins. Small group teaching, pastoral support and SEMH interventions introduced. |
| 4 | Progress | Regular progress reviews. Attendance, engagement and achievement tracked against personal targets. |
| 5 | Transition | Post-placement planning begins. College applications, apprenticeships or return to mainstream arranged. |
| 6 | Destination | 87% move into college, apprenticeships, employment or training. Ongoing alumni support available. |
