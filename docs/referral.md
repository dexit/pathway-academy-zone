## Sections

### The Referral Journey

- **Heading:** The Referral Journey

**Steps:**

| Step | Title | Description |
|------|-------|-------------|
| 1 | Initial Contact | Contact us by phone or complete the referral form to discuss your young person's needs. |
| 2 | Information Gathering | We collect relevant documentation including educational history, EHCP/SEN information, and safeguarding details. |
| 3 | Assessment Meeting | We meet with the young person and family to assess needs and discuss potential pathways. |
| 4 | Placement Planning | We create a personalised plan and agree start dates, transport arrangements, and support packages. |

### Eligibility & Documentation

**Eligibility Criteria:**
- Young people aged 11-16
- Permanently excluded or at risk of exclusion
- Disengaged from mainstream education
- Social, emotional and mental health needs
- Anxiety-based non-attendance
- Looked after children and care leavers

**Documentation Required:**
- Recent school reports and attendance data
- EHCP or SEN support documentation (if applicable)
- Any relevant safeguarding information
- Professional reports (educational psychology, CAMHS, etc.)
- PEP (Personal Education Plan) for looked after children
